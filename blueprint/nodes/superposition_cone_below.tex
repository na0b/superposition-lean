\begin{corollary}[Superposition for comparison with cones from below]
Let $(\mathbf X,d_{\mathbf X})$ and $(\mathbf Y,d_{\mathbf Y})$ be proper geodesic spaces (\noderef{IsGeodesicSpace}), and let $\mathcal U\subsetneq\mathbf X$ and $\mathcal V\subsetneq\mathbf Y$ be domains (\noderef{IsOpenDomain}). Equip $\mathbf X\times\mathbf Y$ with the $\ell^1$ product metric $d_1((x_1,y_1),(x_2,y_2))=d_{\mathbf X}(x_1,x_2)+d_{\mathbf Y}(y_1,y_2)$ and let $\mathcal W=\mathcal U\times\mathcal V$. Let $u:\mathbf X\to\mathbb R$ and $v:\mathbf Y\to\mathbb R$ be such that $u$ is continuous on $\mathcal U$, $v$ is continuous on $\mathcal V$, $u$ satisfies comparison with cones from below in $\mathcal U$, and $v$ satisfies comparison with cones from below in $\mathcal V$ (\noderef{ConeComparisonBelow}). Then $w(x,y)=u(x)+v(y)$ is continuous on $\mathcal W$ and satisfies comparison with cones from below in $\mathcal W$ with respect to $d_1$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{ConeComparisonBelow}, the hypotheses say that $-u$ satisfies comparison with cones from above in $\mathcal U$ and $-v$ satisfies comparison with cones from above in $\mathcal V$ (\noderef{ConeComparisonAbove}). Also $-u$ is continuous on $\mathcal U$ and $-v$ is continuous on $\mathcal V$. All remaining hypotheses (proper geodesic spaces, proper subdomains) are unchanged, so \noderef{superposition_cone_above} applied to $-u,-v$ shows that $\tilde w(x,y)=(-u(x))+(-v(y))$ is continuous on $\mathcal W$ and satisfies comparison with cones from above in $\mathcal W$ with respect to $d_1$. Since $\tilde w=-w$ pointwise, $w=-\tilde w$ is continuous on $\mathcal W$, and $-w=\tilde w$ satisfies comparison with cones from above in $\mathcal W$, which by \noderef{ConeComparisonBelow} means that $w$ satisfies comparison with cones from below in $\mathcal W$.
\end{proof}
