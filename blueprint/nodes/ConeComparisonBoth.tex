\begin{definition}[Comparison with cones from both sides]
Let $(\mathbf X, d_{\mathbf X})$ be a metric space, $\Omega\subseteq\mathbf X$, and let $u$ be a real-valued function defined (at least) on $\Omega$. We say that $u$ \emph{satisfies comparison with cones (from both sides) in $\Omega$} if $u$ satisfies comparison with cones from above in $\Omega$ (\noderef{ConeComparisonAbove}) and $u$ satisfies comparison with cones from below in $\Omega$ (\noderef{ConeComparisonBelow}). In particular such a $u$ is locally bounded (from above and from below) in $\Omega$.
\end{definition}
