\begin{definition}[Comparison with cones from below]
Let $(\mathbf X, d_{\mathbf X})$ be a metric space, $\Omega\subseteq\mathbf X$, and let $u$ be a real-valued function defined (at least) on $\Omega$. We say that $u$ \emph{satisfies comparison with cones from below in $\Omega$} if $-u$ satisfies comparison with cones from above in $\Omega$ in the sense of \noderef{ConeComparisonAbove}. Unfolding that definition, this says: $u$ is locally bounded from below in $\Omega$ (every $x\in\Omega$ has a neighbourhood $t$ with $u$ bounded from below on $t\cap\Omega$), and for any $\hat x\in\Omega$, $c\in\mathbb R$, $\kappa\ge0$ and bounded open $\mathcal O\subset\subset\Omega$ with $\hat x\in\Omega\setminus\mathcal O$, the condition $-u\le c+\kappa\,d_{\mathbf X}(\hat x,\cdot)$ on $\partial\mathcal O$ implies $-u\le c+\kappa\,d_{\mathbf X}(\hat x,\cdot)$ in $\overline{\mathcal O}$.
\end{definition}
