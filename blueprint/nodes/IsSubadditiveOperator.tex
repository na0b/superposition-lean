\begin{definition}[Subadditive operator, paper eq.~(sub-add)]
Let $n\ge0$ and let $\mathbb S^n$ denote the symmetric real $n\times n$ matrices. An operator $L:\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^{n\times n}\to\mathbb R$ is \emph{subadditive} if
\[
L(\xi+\eta,X+Y)\le L(\xi,X)+L(\eta,Y)\qquad\text{for all }\xi,\eta\in\mathbb R^n\text{ and all }X,Y\in\mathbb S^n .
\]
This is the paper's condition (sub-add); only symmetric matrices enter it.
\end{definition}
