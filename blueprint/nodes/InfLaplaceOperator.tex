\begin{definition}[The infinity-Laplace operator]
Let $n\ge0$. The operator $L_\infty:\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^{n\times n}\to\mathbb R$ is
\[
L_\infty(\xi,X)=-\langle X\xi,\xi\rangle ,
\]
where $X\xi$ is the matrix--vector product and $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ the Euclidean inner product. Thus for $\psi$ twice differentiable at $x$, $L_\infty(\nabla\psi(x),\nabla^2\psi(x))=-\langle\nabla^2\psi(x)\nabla\psi(x),\nabla\psi(x)\rangle=-\Delta_\infty\psi(x)$, and a viscosity sub/supersolution of $-\Delta_\infty u=f$ in the paper's sense (Definition def-vis with $L(x,\xi,X)=-\langle X\xi,\xi\rangle-f(x)$, paper lines following def-vis) is a viscosity sub/supersolution of $L_\infty(\nabla u,\nabla^2u)=f$.
\end{definition}
